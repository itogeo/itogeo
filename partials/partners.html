Partners

MSU Geospatial Core Facility

The MSU GCF team pressure-tests our remote sensing, cartography, and Mapbox delivery pipelines. They stress the data, document validation steps, and keep our terrain updates peer-review ready before we share them with counties or corporate innovation teams.

Together we co-author data stories, stand up compute in their lab when projects need extra horsepower, and bring student researchers into production builds so every map feels contemporary and defensible.

Zenith Economics

Our go-to partner for the economics and policy side of geospatial work. Zenith translates our spatial analysis into budget justifications, grant packages, and board-facing briefs — without losing the technical nuance underneath.

We've teamed up on projects like the Montana Airport Economic Impact Study, where their survey design and impact modeling paired with our mapping and StoryMap delivery to give legislators and funders a shared, defensible narrative.

Gallatin Valley Land Trust

GVLT protects working lands, wildlife habitat, and trails across the Gallatin Valley. We build spatial tools with them to support their conservation planning.

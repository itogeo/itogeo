About

I'm Ian Van Dusen. I studied geology at Occidental College, then did NASA-funded remote sensing research on surface-water flooding for my M.S. in Geography at the University of Oregon. I mapped wildfires from the air at Bridger Aerospace, and today I build automated satellite imagery pipelines.

Ito Geospatial is where I bring that work to land trusts, towns, and small teams around Montana, and where I build free tools like Snowline for people who'd rather be outside.
